# LocalWave privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

LocalWave plays audio files you choose. It does not operate a music-upload service, advertising network, or analytics service. The app does not send your music, library, listening history, or account details to the developer.

## Data stored on your device

LocalWave stores your library metadata, artwork, playlists, Favorites, playback settings, selected-folder bookmarks, and cached copies of imported audio in the app’s storage. Bookmarks allow it to reopen files you selected. If iCloud is available, a hash of the system’s iCloud identity token is stored locally to associate sources with the current account; the app does not receive your Apple Account password.

The native macOS app and terminal player store their databases and diagnostic logs locally. Diagnostics may contain file paths and track metadata. They are not automatically sent to the developer.

## Files and iCloud Drive

Files you choose from iCloud Drive or another file provider may be downloaded by that provider through Apple’s system services. Those services follow their own privacy policies. Importing or playing a cloud-backed file can require a network connection. LocalWave does not run its own synchronization server.

## Removing data

Removing a library entry removes its database record; cached audio or artwork may remain in the app’s storage. Deleting the iOS app removes its local app data, subject to your device’s backup and restore settings. This does not delete your original files in Files, Finder or iCloud Drive. Removing the native macOS app’s local app data or the terminal player’s local data folder erases that app’s library and logs.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact options at [olegpustovit.com](https://olegpustovit.com/). Public GitHub issues are visible to others; do not post private music, credentials, or library data there.
