## Downloads

- **LocalWave-VERSION.dmg**: Mac app (Mac Catalyst), Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. Open the DMG and drag LocalWave to Applications. Requires macOS 15 or newer.
- **LocalWave-unsigned.ipa** with its SHA-256 checksum: unsigned, device-only IPA. An unsigned IPA cannot install directly; sign it with your chosen iOS sideloading tool first. Your signing account determines expiry and renewal requirements.

The iPhone and iPad build is distributed to testers through internal TestFlight, not the App Store.

The app requires iOS 18.0 or newer. Imports use folders you select in Files. FLAC tags and artwork are read from native FLAC metadata; local availability is still required for playback.
